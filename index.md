# Privacy Policy — Sudoku Bliss

**Effective date:** October 14, 2026  
**Developer:** jtadev  
**Contact:** jtabel2016@gmail.com

Sudoku Bliss is a Sudoku puzzle game provided by jtadev. This notice describes how information is handled when you use the app.

## Game progress

The app stores puzzle progress, difficulty unlocks, level progress, and hint balances locally on your device using WebView local storage. The app does not require account registration or a game server to play. Local data may be lost if app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

## Advertising and third-party services

Sudoku Bliss includes Google AdMob banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network signals, ad interaction and diagnostic information, and other information subject to their policies and user choices.

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent where required. Ad availability may depend on consent, geography, connectivity, and other factors.

Learn more about Google's practices:
- https://policies.google.com/privacy
- https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

## Your choices

You may choose not to watch rewarded advertisements. Where required, the app provides a Privacy choices control for managing advertising preferences. You may also manage relevant advertising preferences in your device settings.

You may clear locally saved game data through Android's app storage settings; this resets local progress.

## Children

Sudoku Bliss is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children.

## Data sharing, security, and retention

The developer does not operate a separate user-account database for the current game. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information for advertising, fraud prevention, measurement, and diagnostics under their respective policies.

Local progress remains on the device unless Android system backup transfers app data. Third-party retention periods and data practices are controlled by those providers.

## Changes

This policy may be updated to reflect app, SDK, or legal changes. The effective date above will be updated when changes are published.
