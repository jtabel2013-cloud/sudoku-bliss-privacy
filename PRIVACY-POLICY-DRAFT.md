Sudoku Bliss is a Sudoku puzzle game provided by jtadev. This notice describes how information is handled when you use the app.

## Game progress
The app stores puzzle progress, difficulty unlocks, level progress, and hint balances locally on your device using WebView local storage. The current app does not require account registration or a game server to play. Local data may be lost if app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled.

## Advertising and third-party services
Sudoku Bliss includes Google AdMob banner advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location derived from network signals, ad interaction and diagnostic information, and other information subject to their policies and user choices. The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request or manage advertising consent where required. Ad availability may depend on consent, geography, connectivity, and other factors.

Learn more about Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

## Your choices
You may choose not to watch rewarded advertisements. Where required, the app provides a **Privacy choices** control for managing advertising preferences. You may also manage relevant advertising preferences in your device settings. You may clear locally saved game data through Android's app storage settings; this resets local progress.
